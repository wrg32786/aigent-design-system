.aigent/design-direction.md

Approved direction for Saltglass Marine

Variance

A yard ledger

A little unconventional. Not a marina lifestyle brand, not a software landing page.

Motion

Static

No scroll choreography. The type, rules, and ticket carry the page.

Density

Information-dense

Hours, berth, method, and the haul-out form stay on the first long scroll.

  • Themepaper. Rust accent, teal secondary, cream ground.
  • TypeArchivo Narrow for display, Public Sans for body, Martian Mono for tickets and labels.
  • SignatureHorizontal rules, a left-weighted hero, one yard ticket. No three-card grid.
  • CopyOnly supplied facts. No testimonials, ratings, prices, or customer counts.
