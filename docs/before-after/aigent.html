How a job runs

The boat comes out. We inspect it dry and write down what is structural and what is cosmetic. You get a quote before any grind starts. We do the work in the same bay, match the gelcoat to a saved sample, and relaunch on the tide we agreed.

  • 01 HaulBook a week. The yard lifts the boat and blocks it in our bay.
  • 02 InspectMoisture readings, sounding, and a written list of structural versus cosmetic work.
  • 03 QuoteA fixed quote before grinding. If the core is worse than it looked, we stop and re-quote.
  • 04 RepairLaminate, core, through-hulls, then gelcoat matched to the sample we pulled on day one.
  • 05 RelaunchOn the tide written on the ticket, not “when we can.”
