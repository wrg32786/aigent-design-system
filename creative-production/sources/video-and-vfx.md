# Video, VFX, Audio, and Motion Sources

The page needs a visual system, not miscellaneous footage. Acquire media against a brief.

## Free stock

### Pexels and Pixabay

Strong first stops for atmospheric footage, environments, people, and texture. Both permit broad commercial use under their current licenses without mandatory attribution, but restrictions around trademarks, identifiable people, endorsement, and standalone redistribution still apply.

### Mixkit

Useful for footage, music, sound effects, and templates. Check the exact item. Mixkit distinguishes Free and Restricted licenses.

### Coverr

Useful for hero footage and background loops. Recheck current attribution and item terms before production.

### ProductionCrate

Useful for dust, smoke, energy, compositing plates, motion graphics, 3D assets, and sound effects. Current terms distinguish individual, Pro, and enterprise/corporate use. Composite assets into original work and use the plan that matches the organization.

## Paid all-purpose libraries

Use one subscription that matches the actual production volume:

- Envato Elements — broad stock, templates, graphics, audio, and 3D.
- Storyblocks — high-volume footage and motion assets.
- Motion Array — editing templates, motion graphics, plugins, LUTs, footage, and audio.

Pricing and plan rights change. The catalog stores official pricing and license pages rather than promising a fixed price.

## Choosing footage

Prefer clips with:

- one clear subject or material
- slow, stable camera motion
- usable negative space for copy
- consistent lighting
- enough duration to trim a loop
- minimal hard cuts
- no visible brands unless cleared
- a mobile crop that preserves the focal point

Avoid footage that depends on novelty alone. A strong site usually uses one family of footage, one camera language, and one grade.

## VFX and overlays

Use overlays only when they belong to the page's physical world:

- dust in a workshop or archive
- volumetric light in a cinematic room
- fog in an environmental scene
- interface traces in an actual instrumentation system

Do not place particles over every dark page. Composite the overlay into the asset when possible; browser blend modes should be used only when the live response is valuable.

## Audio

Websites should not autoplay audible media. Sound is opt-in and must have a visible control.

Record:

- source and license
- edit or mix performed
- loop points
- loudness target
- fallback when muted
- whether the sound is essential or atmospheric
