# AI Generation Sources

AI generation is one production route, not the design direction. The asset brief comes first.

## Hosted video

### Runway

Useful for controlled image-to-video, generation, and editing. Its free plan is best treated as evaluation. Check the active plan and terms before commercial use.

### Luma Dream Machine

Useful for cinematic generation and modify-video workflows. Under current published guidance, Free and Lite outputs are watermarked and noncommercial; eligible paid plans provide commercial rights.

### Higgsfield

Useful for cinematic camera control, multiple video models, character consistency, and agent-oriented production through CLI or MCP. Pricing and unlimited access vary by region, promotion, and interface. Web allowances may not apply to CLI or MCP generations.

## Local video

### Wan2.2

Official open repository with text-to-video, image-to-video, speech-to-video, and animation variants. Hardware requirements range from high-end consumer GPUs for smaller variants to data-center-class memory for larger models.

### LTX-Video

Official Lightricks repository with local and ComfyUI routes, control workflows, and longer-form options. Verify the exact code and model licenses, plus hardware and disk requirements, before adopting.

### ComfyUI

Use as the repeatable node-based environment around local models. Every checkpoint, LoRA, custom node, and external API has separate terms. Never publish workflows containing credentials or private paths.

## Hosted 3D

Meshy, Tripo, Sloyd, and Rodin can produce useful starting geometry. None should be treated as a one-click web output.

Before commercial use:

1. Check the active plan's output rights.
2. Determine whether the output is public.
3. Record attribution requirements.
4. Save the generation record safely.
5. Clean and optimize the model.
6. Verify the final output in the browser.

## Generation brief

A useful prompt or job packet specifies:

- subject and purpose on the page
- physical material
- environment and lighting
- camera and lens behavior
- motion and duration
- text-safe region
- start and end state
- loop requirement
- prohibited artifacts
- desktop and mobile framing
- continuity references
- output resolution and aspect ratio

Do not prompt with only aesthetic adjectives. Describe the shot and its job.

## Rights and provenance

Generation services can change plans and rights quickly. The exact plan active when the asset was created matters. Record the date, provider, plan class, commercial-use status, and any required attribution in the asset manifest.
