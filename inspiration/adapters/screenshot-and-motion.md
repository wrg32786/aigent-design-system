# Screenshot and Motion Adapter

Screenshots and video are evidence, not a complete implementation specification.

## Screenshot annotation contract

A visual model or reviewer should return:

- visible hierarchy and grouping
- approximate layout topology
- type categories and roles, not invented exact font names
- material and contrast language
- likely interaction states
- visible media and camera treatment
- uncertainty by dimension

Save that record as Design DNA and pass it through `--analysis`.

## Motion filmstrip contract

Sample the beginning, major state changes and ending. Describe:

- what remains fixed
- what moves
- trigger and range
- focal-point movement
- camera or depth behavior
- handoff between states
- mobile replacement
- reduced-motion equivalent

Do not recreate keyframes from a commercial source. Abstract the perceptual purpose and create original timing, direction, subjects and implementation.
