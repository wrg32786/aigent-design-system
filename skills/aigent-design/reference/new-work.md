# New Work

Use for a new surface, major redesign, or a whole page whose composition is still open.

## Establish authority

Classify the target:

- **Established world:** inherit its color, type, material, controls, state, and motion grammar.
- **Incomplete world:** preserve confirmed traits and expand only what the surface needs.
- **Redesign:** preserve product truth and function; replace the discarded visual world completely.
- **Greenfield:** create a world from the audience's real culture, not the category's default landing page.

A missing `DESIGN.md` does not erase a coherent identity already present in production.

## Resolve the job

Record:

- who arrives, where, and in what state of mind
- what they must understand or do
- what only this product can prove
- real content, data, assets, and states
- what must remain untouched
- what would make a polished result feel wrong
- mode: Persuade, Operate, Read, or Experience

## Generate structures before styling

For an open whole surface, generate five to seven materially different structures from the task and content. Use `design-intelligence/layouts.json`, but do not simply list its nearest matches.

Reject:

1. the category default
2. the predictable opposite of the category default
3. any structure that depends on proof or media the product cannot provide

Run the planner. Compare:

- recommended structure
- seeded exploration structure
- conventional fallback

Choose by audience identification and product clarity, not safety.

## Derive a visual world

Name:

- the product's mechanism
- the audience's physical or cultural scene
- concrete artifacts, publications, spaces, tools, rituals, or visual systems that audience recognizes
- the material family
- palette strategy: restrained, committed, full, or drenched
- type character
- primary media system
- one signature interaction

A world is a complete grammar. Carry it through navigation, controls, states, typography, media, and responsive behavior.

## Opening contract

Before implementation, place a short comment at the emitted document root:

```text
THESIS: the one idea and the default arrangement refused.
OWN WORLD: palette, material, type, control language.
STRUCTURE: first viewport and visitor path.
MOTION: one focal motion plus supporting feedback.
PROOF AND FALLBACKS: real evidence, mobile, reduced motion, loading.
```

## Persuade

The offer, proof, and action must remain legible inside the committed form.

## Operate

Expression never obscures task, state, familiar affordance, or repeat use.

## Read

Comprehension, measure, source hierarchy, and wayfinding lead.

## Experience

The work or world leads from the first viewport; interface chrome recedes.
