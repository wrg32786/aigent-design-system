# Color and Material

Color is a spatial and semantic system, not a collection of accents.

## Choose a strategy

- **Restrained** — neutrals plus one signal. Default for Operate and Read.
- **Committed** — one saturated field carries a major region.
- **Full palette** — three or four named roles with clear relationships.
- **Drenched** — color is the environment. Use only when the surface and brand can carry it.

Choose light or dark from the real use scene: who uses this, where, and under what ambient light.

## Define roles

- page ground
- primary and secondary surface
- foreground and muted foreground
- primary action
- current selection
- focus
- success, warning, error, and information
- rules and elevation
- emitted light or media wash, when the world includes it

Secondary text should be tinted from the surface or foreground, not arbitrary gray. Verify actual contrast.

## Material

Every surface should have a reason:

- matte surface for stable content
- raised surface for real elevation or hierarchy
- glass only over valuable media
- border or shadow for elevation, not both by default
- texture only from the chosen physical or cultural world

Do not distribute accent color evenly. Concentration creates hierarchy.

## Stress tests

- bright and dark media frames
- disabled and inactive states
- focus against every surface
- color-blind interpretation of state
- light/dark system preferences when supported
- print or export for decks
- projector and low-contrast display conditions
