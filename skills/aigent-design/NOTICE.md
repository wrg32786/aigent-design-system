What was adapted:

- **scroll-craft**: the feeling curve, single peak, and resolved-ending method; the eight page grammars; the scroll device families and their tuning ranges; scrub, pin, pan, and parallax failure modes (clip-time mapping, lerped playhead, seek-friendly encoding, ground-or-greet, last-section hold); layered-hero depth planning; the signature move and structural fingerprint check; scroll verification by sampling inside each section and a cold feel check.
- **taste-skill**: the explanation of why model copy reads as model copy, the fact sheet, the voice card, the reader-question shape, the per-page device budget, the over-correction table, and the slop word and stock phrase lists (shortened) behind the new Taste copy rules.
- **hallmark**: Floor, Reflex, and Finish tiers with on-the-record waivers; the six-axis pre-build critique and mirror test; the concrete tells in `gates.md` sections 3 and 4; fresh-context hero and finish reviews with first-word verdicts; the native CSS scroll-timeline rules and pinned-chapter pattern in `scroll.md`.
