# The AIgent homepage

**Live surface:** `https://theaigent.xyz`

## Job

Persuade builders that The AIgent is a media company and open-source operating system for people who build with AI, then give them several clear ways to enter: read, run, explore, advertise, or join.

## Structural thesis

The page is a continuous descent through a system rather than a conventional marketing stack.

```text
identity
→ freedom
→ openness
→ operator product
→ human purpose
→ company model
→ pantheon
→ advertiser proof
→ join paths
```

Persistent instrumentation reinforces the metaphor with descent progress, local time, issue state, and sound state. Numbered chapters are justified because the sequence itself carries meaning.

## What is transferable

- one persistent navigation and progress model across a long narrative
- a clear chapter claim before supporting detail
- product proof placed inside the company story rather than isolated in a feature grid
- multiple conversion paths introduced only after the visitor understands the system
- large display type with short supporting copy
- media and motion treated as one continuous environment

## What is brand-specific

- green-black, cream, cyan, and amber palette
- editorial command-center instrumentation
- descent metaphor
- named products, audience, claims, metrics, and calls to action
- The AIgent voice and chapter language

## Mobile contract

- preserve chapter order and primary actions
- reduce persistent instrumentation to what still communicates state
- reframe media around the copy-safe region rather than shrinking the desktop crop
- keep every claim and action usable without autoplay or sound
- flatten spatial travel under reduced motion while preserving chapter distinction

## Extraction

Relevant reusable systems:

- `narrative-descent` layout grammar
- `cinematic-descent` motion system
- `scene-stage` pattern
- `cinematic-page` registry item
- `creative-asset-director` for the environmental media system

## Do not copy

Do not turn every brand page into a numbered dark scroll journey. Use this structure only when the offer benefits from a progressive argument and the visual world can carry one continuous environment.
